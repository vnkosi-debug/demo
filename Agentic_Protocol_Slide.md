# 🚀 Agentic Protocol Engineering: The Wiring Harness of Trust

## **Single Impact Slide: When Arrows Bleed... Trust Leaks Out**

### **🎯 The Production Reality Check**
*Clean diagrams hide dangerous gaps in agentic systems*

**❌ What Fails in Production:**
- ⏰ **Timestamps vanish** between retrieval and reasoning
- 🔍 **Provenance stripped** from critical data
- 🧠 **Memory drifts** without structured handoffs
- ⚡ **Actions fire** without governance checks
- 🔒 **Tools execute** with unchecked privileges

---

### **✅ The Protocol Solution: Trust That Travels**

| **Protocol** | **What It Does** | **Why It Matters** |
|--------------|------------------|-------------------|
| **🧩 MCP**<br/>*Model Context* | Versioned, structured context slots | Models get predictable, validated inputs |
| **💬 ACP**<br/>*Agent Comm* | Traceable message envelopes | Every conversation leaves an audit trail |
| **🤝 A2A**<br/>*Agent-to-Agent* | Task coordination & state sync | Autonomous agents work together safely |
| **🌐 ANP**<br/>*Network* | Cross-org identity & policy | Trust extends beyond enterprise walls |
